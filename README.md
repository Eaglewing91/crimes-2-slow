# Crimes 2.Slow

A Torn userscript made just for a laugh. It adds two unofficial crimes, **Smuggling** and **Assassination**, to the locked slots on the Crimes hub. It's just something to mess about with until a new crime is released officially.

Nothing in it is real. No nerve is spent, no money or items are gained, and your actual crime stats are never touched. Your progress is kept only in your own browser.

## Install

1. Install [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/).
2. Open [crimes-2-slow.user.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Eaglewing91/crimes-2-slow/main/crimes-2-slow.user.js) and click **Install**.
3. Go to the Crimes hub in Torn. Smuggling and Assassination appear where the first two locked crimes were.

## The crimes

**Smuggling:** pick a cargo and a route (Road, Rail, Sea, Air or Courier), dispatch it, then collect it when it arrives. Routes build up heat the more you use them, and better cargo unlocks as you level.

**Assassination:** accept a contract, then work through four steps: Watch, Arm, Close In and Strike. Learn the target's routine, choose the right weapon and strike while they're exposed. Targets unlock at levels 1, 25, 50 and 75.

## Rules

The script follows Torn's scripting rules:

- It makes no network requests of any kind, to Torn or anywhere else.
- It never clicks anything on Torn's pages. Every click only does one thing inside the script's own page.
- The crime tiles are plain links that load a page in the same tab.
